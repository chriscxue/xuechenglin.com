VIBRATION

Visualization of sound data
Digital Print
Arduino, Sound Sensor

Perhaps in the everyday world, sound is the representative of things which are appeared or disappeared. This is a silent world.

This print is based on sound data visualized by Arduino. As we know, humans are using their limited senses (sight, touch, smell, taste, and hearing) to change and build their worlds. The series of prints is an experiment in feeling sound through sight, attempting to interchange the senses to surpass senses.

声音是另一个事物出现或消失的代表，这是一个沉默的世界
