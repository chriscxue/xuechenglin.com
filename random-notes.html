RANDOM NOTES

30X30 in
Black & White, Digital Print

Roll a pair of dice 2,500 times. Record the number given with each roll of the dice on a 9×9 grid. Project the image on a 30 x 30 grid until it is filled, producing a patterned image.

将骰子的每个面用九宫格代表, 掷一次骰子就将得到的数字记录在纸上的九宫格。 每经过2500次的投掷便得到一张图像,这些记录了选择与被选择过程的图像代表了什么?
